About Me

Detail-oriented Computer Applications scholar currently pursuing my Master of Computer Applications (MCA, 2025–Present) at Bhilai Institute of Technology, Durg (BIT Durg), with a completed Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA, 2022–2025) from Korba Computer College (ABVV University), Chhattisgarh.
