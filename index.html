About Me

Enthusiastic and detail-oriented Computer Applications undergraduate (BCA, expected 2025) at Korba Computer College (ABVV), Chhattisgarh, with hands-on experience in cybersecurity, web development, and technical mentorship.

I have built and contributed to several impactful projects, including a full-stack phishing detection platform that leverages Python, Streamlit, and threat intelligence APIs like VirusTotal, integrating features such as real-time port scanning, SSL/TLS checks, and community-driven reporting. My open-source work also includes a comprehensive e-learning platform and a dynamic e-commerce website, both developed with a strong focus on security, user experience, and scalable architecture.

I am recognized for my ability to simplify complex technical concepts, having been honored as "Best Assistant Professor as Student" for my mentorship and teaching support. My technical toolkit includes JavaScript, NodeJS, React, Tailwind, MongoDB, MySQL, PHP, C++, Linux, Git, and OSINT methodologies.

I am passionate about building secure, user-centric applications and thrive in collaborative environments where learning and innovation are prioritized. I am always eager to connect with like-minded professionals and explore opportunities to contribute to meaningful tech solutions.
